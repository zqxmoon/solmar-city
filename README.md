Solmar City is an original open-world crime game: explore a sun-soaked coastal city on foot or by car, steal rides, outrun the Solmar PD, run three story missions for Dana Reyes, and collect cash across the streets, parks and beach.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network requests, just static files. Controls: WASD move/drive, E steal & enter cars, mouse or Space shoot, Shift run, Esc pause, M mute. Progress saves automatically in your browser.
